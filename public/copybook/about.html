About

Holdings as filed. Nothing else.

Copybook is a consumer 13F site from Factor Dynamics. It reads each manager's latest 13F-HR information table from SEC EDGAR and shows issuer, CUSIP, shares, and the filer-reported value.

What you are looking at

  • Public 13F-HR filings, as filed. Fair-access User-Agent, under 10 requests/second.
  • CUSIP to ticker from a static map when we have one. Missing tickers show as a dash, not a guessed price.
  • Split 13F lines (same CUSIP, several other-manager rows) are summed.
  • The copycat book is the value-weighted sum of those as-filed books. It is not a fund.

What we do not show

  • Live quotes, sparklines, or “up 2% this week.”
  • Yahoo or any unlicensed price data.
  • Form 4 or 13D. Those parsers are empty; this launch is holdings-only.
  • Dataroma HTML. Managers are the 41 hard-coded CIKs.

Lag and holes

13F is due 45 days after quarter-end. It omits shorts, many options, and advisers under $100M AUM. Values are the filer's quarter-end marks, not today's market.

Not investment advice. Factor Dynamics is not a registered investment adviser. Past research results are not a live track record.
